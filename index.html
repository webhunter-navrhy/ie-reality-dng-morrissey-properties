Description

Prime location! …… This three bedroomed home comes to the market with its fantastic location, a mature and ever popular neighbourhood only a stroll away from, The Waterford Greenway, Eurospar Grocery Shop, and ten minutes walk from Dungarvan town centre, including Dungarvan Shopping Centre, Restaurants, Bars, Walton Park, and seaside town walks, all literally on the doorstep. The accommodation is deceptively spacious and needing renovation throughout, but presents a great opportunity at this location for a range of potential purchasers. Spacious accommodation comprises of, Entrance Porch, Entrance Hallway, Sitting room, Living Room, Kitchen, Dining Room, Garage Room, three Bedrooms and Bathroom. The property also has the benefit of a private and sunny south side rear garden, front garden with off street parking to the front, and accessed by a small cul de sac roadway accessing only three other houses.

Properties like this one don’t come to the open market that often in Dungarvan town, and DNG Morrissey properties highly recommend early viewing.
